Privacy

Draft for the private beta

This draft describes what Sandwich is built to do. It is not legal advice and will be reviewed before the beta opens. Anything marked TBD is not decided yet.

What we collect

  • Hosts: the name and email your Google or GitHub account shares when you sign in.
  • Guests: the name you type when you join. No account, no email.
  • Session content: what people type, what the agent says and does, the project's files, and the saved memory for the project.
  • Usage numbers: turns, tokens and estimated cost, credited to the person who started each turn.
  • Technical logs: to keep the service running. Logs never contain keys or invite links.

Who can see it

  • Everyone in a session can read everything in it, including the host.
  • The AI company you choose (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) receives what the agent is asked and answers, under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • We look at session content only if needed to fix a problem or stop abuse. TBD: the exact rule and how we record it.

Your API key

A key you enter is held only while the session runs. It is encrypted, kept outside the sandbox where the agent runs, and deleted when the session ends. TBD: confirm the exact deletion timing once built.

Cookies

One cookie keeps hosts signed in. There is no advertising and no tracking, and we load nothing from other websites. Your browser remembers your guest name on your own device.

How long we keep things

Deleting a session deletes its chat, files and memory. TBD: the default retention period, and how long backups last.

Your choices

  • Delete any session you host, at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it.
  • Ask what we hold about you. TBD: the process and timing.
