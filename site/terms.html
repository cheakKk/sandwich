This draft explains how Sandwich is meant to work. It is not legal advice and will be reviewed by a lawyer before the beta opens. Anything marked TBD is not decided yet.

What Sandwich is

Sandwich lets a host run a shared session with an AI agent and invite other people to watch it, steer it and hand it off. The agent is provided by an AI company you choose (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) using your own account key.

Your account and your key

  • Hosts sign in with Google or GitHub. Guests join with a single-use invite link and need no account.
  • You use your own API key. You pay the AI company directly for what the agent uses. Sandwich does not sell or resell model usage.
  • You are responsible for your key and for what is done with it in your sessions.

Sessions and the people you invite

  • Everyone in a session can read everything said in it. Do not invite people you would not show the whole conversation to.
  • The host is responsible for the people they invite and for what the agent does on their account.
  • Guests steer the agent with the host's key. The host can set daily limits per guest and a budget for the session.

What you may not do

  • Break the law, or use Sandwich to attack, scan or harm other people or systems.
  • Use the sandboxes to mine cryptocurrency, send spam, or relay attacks.
  • Try to escape a sandbox, read another person's session, or bypass the limits.

We may pause or end a session or an account that breaks these rules.

AI output and approvals

AI agents make mistakes. Sandwich asks a person to approve what matters, but it cannot promise that the agent will never do something unwanted. You are responsible for what you approve and what you run.
